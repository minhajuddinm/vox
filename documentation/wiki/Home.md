# Vox

Push-to-talk dictation for **Windows** and **Android**. Hold a shortcut (Windows) or tap a floating bubble (Android), speak, and cleaned-up text is typed into the app you are using. Speech-to-text and cleanup run on Groq's free tier by default, or on any OpenAI-compatible server you choose, with your own key. No Vox server, no account, no telemetry.
