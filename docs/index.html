Vox

Free voice dictation for Windows and Android

Hold a shortcut, speak, and cleaned-up text appears wherever your cursor is. Take meeting notes with a transcript, summary, action items and who said what. Vox runs on your PC or phone and works with Groq's free speech and AI models and your own key, or any OpenAI-compatible server.

Download (Windows and Android) Source code
Dictation. Hold Ctrl + Win to talk, release to insert. Double-tap for hands-free. Filler words removed, self-corrections applied, punctuation fixed, tone matched to the app.
Meeting notes. Records your mic and your PC's audio. No bot joins the call. Optional Google Calendar connection adds meeting titles and attendee names.
Your data goes only where you point it. Vox has no analytics and no server of its own. Audio and text go to the speech and cleanup servers you choose (Groq by default) and, if you turn it on, to a relay you run yourself. Settings, history and notes are stored on your device. See the privacy policy.
